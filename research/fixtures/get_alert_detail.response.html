Description:
The connection to the devices on port 10 has been lost.

Solution:
  • If the cable has been removed from the port, reattach the cable.
  • If the device attached by the cable is not active/on, troubleshoot that device.
  • If the port has been disabled on the other device, re-enable the port on the other device.

Other Possibilities:
The cable is damaged or severed. If so, replace the cable.
